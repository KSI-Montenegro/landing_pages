One Seamless Educational Journey for Your Child

Every stage of education at KSI Montenegro builds naturally on the one before. Your child does not need to adapt to a new system or teaching philosophy as they grow. We develop knowledge, confidence, and character step by step, from their very first day through to graduation.

Early Years & Primary (Ages 3–11)

PYP — Primary Years Programme

Children are naturally curious. Our goal is to channel that curiosity, not suppress it. Through inquiry-based learning and six globally significant themes, your child develops critical thinking, creativity, and a genuine love of learning from the very beginning.

Middle School (Ages 11–16)

MYP — Middle Years Programme

This is the stage when young people start asking why things matter. The MYP gives them real answers. Subjects connect to the real world, critical thinking becomes a daily habit, and students begin to understand their own strengths and define who they want to become.

Pre-University (Ages 16–18)

IB Diploma Programme

The IB Diploma is the gold standard of international education, recognised at over 5,000 universities worldwide. Our graduates earn places at universities in Italy, France, Spain, the Netherlands, and the United States — not by chance, but because they are better prepared than their peers.

Beyond Academic Achievement

Global Citizens from Day One

Your child will grow and learn alongside peers from over 30 countries. That experience cannot be taught from a textbook. It is built every day, in the classroom, during breaks, and through collaborative projects. The friendships and perspectives formed here last a lifetime.

Whole-Child Development

Education at KSI Montenegro does not stop when the bell rings. Sport, arts, and extracurricular activities are not optional extras. They are core to our programme. A child who develops only academically is not fully prepared for life, and we build both.

A World-Class Campus

Modern laboratories, creative studios, and sports facilities are designed to support contemporary, interactive teaching. Your child spends eight hours a day here. The environment matters as much as what they learn inside it.

The Only CIS Accredited School in Montenegro

CIS accreditation from the Council of International Schools is not a marketing claim. It is independent verification by international experts that KSI Montenegro meets the highest global standards in teaching, school governance, and student welfare. No other school in Montenegro holds this recognition.
